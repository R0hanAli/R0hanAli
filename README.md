## 👋 A little about me

I'm Rohan, a developer who enjoys taking an idea from a rough concept and turning it into something people can actually interact with.

Most of my time goes into **Flutter**, **backend development**, and **full stack web applications**. I like working across the whole product rather than staying on just one side of the stack.

🌱 Currently exploring **AI, automation, backend architecture, and scalable applications**

🔨 Building with **Flutter, Node.js, React, Firebase, Supabase, and REST APIs**

💡 Interested in **SaaS products, mobile apps, developer tools, and real world software**

🧩 I enjoy solving problems where the UI, backend, database, and APIs all have to work together

☕ Most of my best debugging sessions start with "this should work"
